# Robust Factored Safe Policy Improvement with Baseline Bootstrapping

Safe Policy Improvement with Baseline Bootstrapping (SPIBB)
constrains policy improvement in poorly supported regions of an
offline dataset by reverting to a baseline policy.

Factored SPIBB exploits conditional independence in transition
dynamics to improve sample efficiency.

Robust MDPs instead represent model uncertainty through sets of
plausible transition distributions.

## Research Question

Can persistent transition-model ambiguity be incorporated at the
factor level into SPIBB while retaining a baseline-relative safe
policy improvement guarantee?
