## Current Research Question

> Can persistent transition-model ambiguity be incorporated at the factor
> level into Factored SPIBB while retaining a baseline-relative safe policy
> improvement guarantee?

> Can factor-level ambiguity make robust safe policy improvement less
> conservative or more sample-efficient than an unfactored robust approach?

> The proposed direction combines:

1. baseline-relative safe policy improvement;
2. baseline bootstrapping;
3. factored transition structure;
4. robust transition ambiguity.
