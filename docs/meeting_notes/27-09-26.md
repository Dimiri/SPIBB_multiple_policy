## Preliminaries

- $\rho(\pi_i) \geq \rho(\pi_{i-1})$ does **not** mean:
  $$
  V^{\pi_i}(s) \geq V^{\pi_{i-1}}(s) \quad \forall s
  $$
- Bootstrapping set:
  $$
  B_D = \{(s,a) : N_D(s,a) < N_\wedge\}
  $$
  For $(s,a) \in B_D$:
  $$
  \pi(a|s) = \pi_{\text{ref}}(a|s)
  $$
- What is a Semi-MDP (sMDP)?
  - An option can span multiple MDP time steps:
    $$
    s_t \xrightarrow{o} s_{t+k}
    $$
  - An option is defined as:
    $$
    o = \langle I, \pi, \beta \rangle
    $$
